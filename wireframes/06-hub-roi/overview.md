# SCR-06 — HUB ROI

## Purpose

Show adoption, alert-to-action conversion, decision time, costs, benefits, and payback with financial validation.

## First viewport

- Adoption active.
- Action over alerts.
- Time to decision.
- Estimated benefit.
- Validated benefit.
- Platform cost and ROI.

## Primary actions

Inspect benefit, review assumptions, attach evidence, validate value, and compare estimated versus realized value.

## Guardrail

Estimated, influenced, validated, and realized value must remain distinct.
