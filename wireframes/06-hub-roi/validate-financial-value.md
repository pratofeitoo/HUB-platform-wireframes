# WF-06B — Validate financial value

## Finance review

- Linked decision and action.
- Beneficiary, lever, period, and intervention.
- Estimated amount and premises.
- Financial source and reconciliation status.
- Evidence attachments.
- Duplicate-counting check.
- Finance decision and reason.

## Outcomes

`Validated`, `Needs evidence`, `Adjusted`, `Rejected`, or `Deferred`.

The UI must prevent a validated value from being recorded without evidence and an identified Finance reviewer.
