# WF-06A — Estimated versus realized value

## Purpose

Prevent the platform from presenting a forecast or estimate as proven financial return.

## Comparison layout

| Measure | Amount | State | Evidence | Owner |
|---|---:|---|---|---|
| Estimated value | R$ — | Estimated | Premises and model version | Decision owner |
| Influenced value | R$ — | Influenced | Attribution record | HUB / Finance |
| Validated value | R$ — | Validated | Finance evidence | Finance |
| Realized value | R$ — | Realized | Reconciled financial source | Finance |
| HUB cost | R$ — | Reconciled | Cost source | Finance |

## Actions

Review premises, attach evidence, submit for Finance validation, and open audit history.

## Traceability

`SCR-06`, `BL-015`, `FLD-022`, `FLD-023`, `GOV-09`.
