# SCR-04 — KPI Governance

## Purpose

Allow authorized users to define, review, version, approve, and trace governed KPIs.

## First viewport

- KPI catalog with status and owner.
- Draft, review, approved, and retired filters.
- Formula, source, target, threshold, and lineage summary.

## Primary actions

Propose, edit, submit, approve, reject, compare versions, and open lineage.

## Traceability

`SCR-04`, `BL-006`, `GOV-06`, `GOV-09`.
