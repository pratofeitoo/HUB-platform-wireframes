# WF-01C — Driver analysis

## Purpose

Help the executive inspect the signals associated with a KPI movement and understand what requires human review.

## Content

- Selected KPI and comparison period.
- Contributing dimensions or related signals.
- Source and calculation method for each signal.
- Confidence and data-quality indicator.
- “Not a causal conclusion” notice where applicable.
- Related alerts and open decisions.

## Actions

Open alert, inspect source, simulate scenario, or assign a follow-up decision.
