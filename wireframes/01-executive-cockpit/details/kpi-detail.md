# WF-01B — KPI detail

## Purpose

Explain one KPI without requiring the user to infer meaning from a number or chart.

## Content

- KPI name and ID.
- Current value, target, threshold, direction, and trend.
- Formula and definition.
- Granularity and selected period.
- Source systems and freshness.
- Lineage link.
- Authorized dimensions for drill-down.
- Caveats, confidence, and limitations.

## Actions

Open driver analysis, change authorized scope, export governed result, or return to cockpit.

## Guardrail

Do not show a causal interpretation unless supported by approved evidence. Use “associated signals” where appropriate.
