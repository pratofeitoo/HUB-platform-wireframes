# SCR-01 — Executive Cockpit

## Purpose

Give executives and sponsors a fast, aggregated view of business performance, people signals, client exposure, scenarios, and HUB value.

## First viewport

- Scope and freshness bar.
- Revenue, margin, people, client-risk, and ROI KPI cards.
- Priority risks and decisions queue.
- Trend or scenario panel.

## Primary actions

Open driver, simulate scenario, approve decision, and inspect authorized evidence.
