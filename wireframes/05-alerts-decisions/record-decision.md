# WF-05C — Record decision

## Required fields

- Decision: accept, modify, reject, defer, suppress, or escalate.
- Reason.
- Context or evidence.
- Responsible person.
- Due date or SLA response.
- Expected outcome.
- Optional estimated value.

## Confirmation

Before submission, show the affected alert, actor, timestamp, new status, and audit consequence.

## Guardrail

The interface must not imply that the system made the decision. The decision belongs to the named human actor.
