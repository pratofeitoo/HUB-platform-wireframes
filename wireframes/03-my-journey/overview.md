# SCR-03 — My Journey

## Purpose

Give the employee a clear view of goals, check-ins, feedback, skills, and development actions.

## First viewport

- Current cycle and progress.
- Goal status.
- Upcoming check-in.
- Recent feedback.
- Development plan progress.

## Primary actions

Update goal, submit check-in, respond to feedback, inspect source, and contest information.
