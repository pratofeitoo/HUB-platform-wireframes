# Review checklist

- [ ] Screen traces to `SCR-01..09`, a module, KPI, alert, backlog item, or governance rule.
- [ ] Intended profiles and allowed actions are explicit.
- [ ] Loading, empty, stale, partial, error, and denied states are covered.
- [ ] Source and last-updated information are visible.
- [ ] Recommendations show explanation, limits, and human review.
- [ ] Sensitive information is minimized or masked.
- [ ] Decisions and financial validations produce an audit trail.
- [ ] Sample content is synthetic and labelled illustrative.
