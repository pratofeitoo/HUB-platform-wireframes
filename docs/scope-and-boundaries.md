# Scope and boundaries

## In scope

- MVP screen map and role-based navigation.
- Executive, Manager, Employee, Finance, HUB, and Data/Admin journeys.
- Seven MVP destination screens: `SCR-01` through `SCR-07`.
- Supporting alert, decision, action, KPI, ROI, and data-quality states.
- Synthetic sample data and an interactive prototype.

## Deferred

- `SCR-08` Supplier Marketplace: MVP+1.
- Academic, communities, and future talent capabilities: later phases.
- Production integrations, live model decisions, and real personal data.

## Boundary

This workspace may propose interface behavior. It may not change the canonical technical requirements, KPI definitions, legal basis, retention policy, or authorization model without an explicit recorded decision.
